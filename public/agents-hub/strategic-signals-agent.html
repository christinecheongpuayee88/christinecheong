Under the Hood

Orchestrated diversity of thought

Your data passes through twelve analytical lenses across two squads. One filters for strict importance — consequence, constraint, and risk. The other hunts for genuine interest — counter-intuition, human truth, and creative outliers. A critic layer ensures quality.

⚠️
Important Squad
Consequence, risk & bottom-line impact
  • Decision Impact
    What would change a plan, budget, or timeline?
  • Constraint Detector
    Non-negotiable, legal, or politically delicate
  • Risk Scanner
    What could fail, backfire, or create exposure?
  • Evidence Weigher
    Well-supported vs. suspiciously unsupported
  • Stakeholder Pressure
    Who has power & whose needs are missing?
  • Commercial Consequence
    Revenue, retention, cost & competitive edge
⟶
✨
Interesting Squad
Novelty, curiosity & divergent patterns
  • Counter-Intuition
    Cuts against category assumptions
  • Human Truth
    Emotion, identity, anxiety & desire
  • Language Clues
    Words, metaphors & omissions that reveal priorities
  • Cultural Signal
    Emerging behaviour, norms & tensions
  • Creative Outlier
    Small detail that could inspire a big idea
  • Productive Contradiction
    Two things that cannot both be fully true
⟶
🔎
Critic Layer
Quality assurance & hallucination check
  • Relevance Gate
    Filters noise, ensures focus
  • Hallucination Checker
    Verifies grounding in source data
