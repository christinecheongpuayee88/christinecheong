Autonomous Research Flow
1. Strategist interprets your brief and assigns specialist roles
2. Six Specialists perform parallel web research from unique angles
3. Compiler synthesises findings into an executive intelligence report

Under the Hood

Continuous autonomous operation

This isn't a chatbot conversation — it's a chain reaction. A Strategist designs the plan, six Specialists execute in parallel, and a Compiler delivers the final report.

01

Strategist

Interprets your brief, identifies the most productive research angles, and assigns six specialist roles — each with a unique direction to follow.

02

Six Specialists

Each specialist performs independent web research, analyses sources, and authors a formally structured report from their assigned perspective.

03

Compiler

Aggregates all specialist findings into a single executive summary, focusing on the most critical and strategically relevant insights.
