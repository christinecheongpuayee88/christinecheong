Five Modes

One agent, five ways to use it

Each mode is tuned for a different stage of the insight and innovation process — from early trend scanning to concept validation.

👤

Talk to Consumers

Interact with simulated personas that match your target audience. Each stays internally consistent in attitudes, motivations, and constraints.

  • Natural persona responses
  • Emotional & practical drivers
  • Says vs. likely does
📈

Trend Analysis

Synthesise research inputs into actionable trend digests — themes, drivers, consumer implications, brand opportunities, and risks.

  • Key trend themes
  • Consumer & brand implications
  • Recommended next moves
💡

Concept Testing

Evaluate a concept, proposition, or campaign through persona lenses — appeal, clarity, distinctiveness, barriers, and improvement opportunities.

  • Structured evaluation
  • Likely verbatim reactions
  • Improvement opportunities
👥

Panel Comparison

See how multiple distinct personas respond to the same stimulus — find common patterns, sharp differences, and best-fit audiences.

  • Persona-by-persona reactions
  • Strongest & weakest fit
  • Segment-specific actions
📋

Research Design

Generate interview guides, survey questions, screening logic, and learning agendas to validate ideas with real consumers.

  • Discussion guides
  • Survey frameworks
  • Test plan structure
