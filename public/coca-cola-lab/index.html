Lab Steps & Copyable Prompts

One connected case, six prompting techniques. Copy each prompt into ChatGPT or Gemini, verify the output and make human decisions at every checkpoint. All data and external sources must be supplied by your trainer.

Scenario & deliverables

As an analyst, investigate which factors are associated with variations in Units Sold in the Coca-Cola 2022 dataset. Begin with an analytical plan, enrich the data, calculate internal evidence, then use approved external PDFs and stakeholder challenge to inform commercial actions.

Carry forward: Approved analytical plan → chosen enriched/original CSV → internal findings → cited external synthesis → stakeholder perspectives → analyst-owned commercial recommendations.

Before you start

Have the original Coca-Cola CSV, a chatbot capable of working with CSV/PDF files, and the group worksheet.

Rule: Human decides, AI assists. Never treat a correlation as proof of causation.

Activity 1

RCTF — Frame the Analysis

15 min

Write the business question and propose a plan; do not calculate results.

Use: ChatGPT or Gemini · original Coca-Cola CSV

Lab steps

View lab steps
  1. Read the Coca-Cola 2022 dataset and agree the outcome: Units Sold.
  2. Independently write one sentence explaining which commercial decision needs support.
  3. Upload the original CSV, paste the prompt and submit.
  4. Review the plan for relevant brand, retailer, region, month, price and delivery dimensions; request changes before approving.
1. Propose an analytical plan (RCTF)
Prompt · upload the original Coca-Cola CSV, then paste into ChatGPT or Gemini
Role: Act as a business analyst.
Context: Use the attached dataset.
Task: Propose an analytical plan to investigate changes in [business outcome — analyse sales volume drivers], covering key measures, comparisons and potential factors.
Format: Present a concise plan using WHAT → WHY → VALIDATE.
Do not perform the analysis yet.
✓ Initial WHAT → WHY → VALIDATE plan; learner checks scope and approves.
✓ Check the result: A concise WHAT → WHY → VALIDATE analytical plan; human approves or revises it.

Human checkpoint: Gate A — approve initial plan. Approve / Revise / Stop. Do not continue until decided.

Activity 2

Ask Before Answer — Refine the Plan

15 min

Use clarifying questions to improve the analysis scope without executing analysis.

Use: Same AI conversation · approved initial plan

Lab steps

View lab steps
  1. Keep the initial plan in the same conversation.
  2. Paste the prompt, answer up to three questions and consider whether each would materially improve the analysis.
  3. Review the revised plan and compare it with the initial plan.
  4. Approve the revised plan only when the scope and validations are clear.
2. Clarify and refine the analytical plan
Prompt · use the same AI conversation; answer the questions before revising
Review the proposed analytical plan.
1. Ask up to three clarifying questions to improve its business relevance, scope and analytical approach. Wait for my answers.
2. Based on my responses, produce a revised analytical plan covering:
   • WHAT: Measures, trends and comparisons to examine.
   • WHY: Potential factors and relationships to investigate.
   • VALIDATE: Checks to ensure reliable findings.
3. Highlight key improvements and assumptions.
Output: Revised Analytical Plan | Key Improvements | Assumptions.
Do not execute the analysis, calculate results, generate charts or draw conclusions. Return only the proposed analytical plan and wait for my approval.
✓ Revised plan, clarified assumptions and recorded approval.
✓ Check the result: Revised plan, improvements and assumptions; human approves or revises.

Human checkpoint: Gate B — approve revised plan. Approve / Revise / Stop. Do not continue until decided.

Activity 4

CoT — Internal Evidence (WHAT → WHY → VALIDATE)

30 min

Choose the analysis dataset first; then calculate and validate internal findings. No external research.

Use: Selected CSV · approved analytical plan

Lab steps

View lab steps
  1. Review the original and enriched CSV. Run Prompt 4 to compare them, then explicitly choose which dataset to use.
  2. Attach the chosen CSV and refer to the approved analytical plan.
  3. Run Prompt 5 to calculate WHAT patterns, WHY associations and VALIDATE checks using only the selected internal dataset.
  4. Review numerical evidence, visuals, data quality, coverage and limitations; approve or revise the internal findings before Activity 5.
4. Select the dataset for CoT analysis
Prompt · choose which verified CSV will be used for CoT
For the next CoT internal analysis, should I use (A) the enriched dataset or (B) the original dataset? Briefly compare the available columns and any implications for the approved analytical plan. Do not start the analysis until I choose.
✓ Dataset choice recorded explicitly before CoT.
5. Analyse internal evidence (CoT)
Prompt · attach the chosen dataset and use the approved plan
Act as a business analyst. Analyse the attached dataset step by step, following the approved analytical plan.

1. WHAT — Analyse & Visualise
Explore relevant dimensions and their combinations to identify meaningful patterns and differences in [business outcome — Units Sold]. Support key findings with KPIs, tables and charts.

2. WHY — Investigate Associations
Use group comparisons to explore factors associated with the key findings. Identify meaningful relationships supported by numerical evidence.

3. VALIDATE — Check Evidence
Verify calculations, check data quality and identify limitations or alternative explanations.

Output: Summarise the most important findings using WHAT → WHY → VALIDATE, with supporting evidence and visualisations where useful. Include a concise log of dimensions and combinations checked, and any exclusions.

Use only the supplied dataset. Do not invent findings or assume associations imply causation.
✓ Numerical findings, visuals, limitations and coverage checked by learner.
✓ Check the result: Dataset choice recorded before analysis; approved WHAT → WHY → VALIDATE evidence and coverage log.

Human checkpoints: First choose the original or enriched dataset (Prompt 4); then approve internal findings after Prompt 5 (Gate C). Approve / Revise / Stop.

Activity 5

Prompt Chaining — Ground, Synthesise, Verify

40 min

Use sequential prompts to reconcile internal and external evidence, with citations.

Use: Approved CoT findings + six approved external PDFs

Lab steps

Approved external source checklist (six PDFs)
  • Coca Cola Annual Report 2022.pdf
  • coca-cola-business-and-sustainability-report-2022.pdf
  • CollageGroup_Generations_Beverage_CategoryEssentials_Fall2022.pdf
  • US CPI 2022.pdf
  • YouGov-FMCG-rankings-report-2023.pdf
  • WP-2023-09-US-Grocery-Trends-Report.pdf

The class may use another source set only with explicit trainer/user approval.

View lab steps
  1. Prepare the approved internal findings. Confirm which external PDFs the trainer has supplied; do not add unapproved sources.
  2. Prompt 5A (GROUND): extract matching external evidence and sources; mark nonmatches.
  3. Prompt 5B (SYNTHESISE): interpret where evidence agrees, differs or is inconclusive.
  4. Prompt 5C (VERIFY): inspect source dates, figures, citations and unsupported claims; correct synthesis.
  5. Review the final evidence table and retain gaps for the stakeholder activity.
6. Ground insights in external sources
Prompt · supply only the six approved external PDFs and approved internal findings
Using the approved internal analytical findings, identify relevant evidence from the supplied external sources.
Extract evidence that may support, challenge or provide context for the findings. Do not synthesise or infer explanations yet.

Output: Internal Finding | External Evidence | Source.

Cite the approved documents and pages where possible. Use only approved sources. Do not invent evidence.
✓ Relevant source evidence with traceable references; gaps stated explicitly.
7. Synthesise internal and external evidence
Prompt · continue from the grounded evidence table
Using the approved internal findings and the external evidence extracted in Prompt 6, synthesise the key insights. Do not search for new sources or repeat the evidence-extraction step.

Explain where the evidence agrees, differs or remains inconclusive.

Output: Key Finding | Evidence Synthesis | Business Implications | Limitations.

Preserve supporting figures and source references. Distinguish supported explanations from hypotheses. Do not assume association implies causation.
✓ Agreement, disagreement and unknowns remain clearly distinguished.
8. Verify the evidence synthesis
Prompt · check the synthesis against source files and the internal analysis
Verify the synthesis against the attached internal analysis and approved external sources.

Check important figures, citations, contradictions and unsupported claims. Revise the synthesis where necessary.

Output: Verified Insights | Evidence Strength | Remaining Uncertainties.

Do not introduce new evidence or invent findings.
✓ Figures, citations and unsupported claims reviewed and corrected.
✓ Check the result: Three linked tables, preserving citations, uncertainties and a reviewed source-coverage log.

Human checkpoint: Gate D — approve external synthesis. Approve / Revise / Stop. Do not continue until decided.

Activity 6

Perspective Prompting — Stakeholder Challenge

20 min

Examine 4–6 internal and external viewpoints without inventing stakeholder testimony.

Use: Verified synthesis + approved internal and external evidence

Lab steps

View lab steps
  1. Attach or reference the approved synthesis and source data.
  2. Paste the stakeholder-panel prompt. Check that both commercial teams and external consumer/trade viewpoints are included.
  3. Discuss which alternative explanations genuinely challenge the evidence.
  4. The GROUP—not AI—consolidates the findings into the final commercial task below.
9. Challenge insights from stakeholder perspectives
Prompt · use the verified synthesis; treat views as simulated
Act as a panel of 4–6 relevant internal and external business stakeholders including consumers and trade customers.

Review the evidence-based synthesis using the attached internal analysis and external sources.

For each stakeholder identify alternative interpretations, concerns and business implications. Identify areas of agreement, disagreement and insights requiring further validation.

Output: Stakeholder | Perspective | Key Concerns | Business Implications.

Ground the assessment in the supplied evidence. Treat simulated stakeholder views as hypotheses, not verified opinions.
✓ 4–6 stakeholder perspectives challenged; group decides the final interpretation.
✓ Check the result: Stakeholder matrix with disagreement and validation needs; human team chooses its conclusions.

Human checkpoint: Gate E — approve stakeholder assessment. Approve / Revise / Stop. Do not continue until decided.
