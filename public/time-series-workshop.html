Part 1 · Univariate forecasting

Forecast from the series itself

Use past values, shocks, differencing and repeating seasonal patterns to forecast the future.

01

Make the series stationary

Use regular or seasonal differencing when the level is not stable.

02

Read ACF and PACF

Use non-seasonal and seasonal lags to identify tentative AR and MA structure.

03

Validate the model

Look for white-noise residuals and compare plausible models using several criteria.

Part 2 · Multivariate forecasting

Add external drivers

Combine the target's own time structure with external information that may lead its movement.

Predictive relationship ≠ causal proof. ARIMAX can improve forecasting, but does not establish causality by itself.
01

Find leading indicators

Advertising, promotions or operational inputs may add forecast information.

02

Model delayed effects

An input may influence Y later and across several lags.

03

Check future X

External-variable values must be known, planned or forecast.

Reusable decision aid

What forecasting process should I follow?

1 · Understand & Prepare

Define the target, forecast horizon and decision.
Explore patterns and prepare the data appropriately.

2 · Build & Validate

Select suitable forecasting approaches.
Compare performance on unseen time periods and diagnose errors.

3 · Forecast & Apply

Generate forecasts, uncertainty ranges and scenarios.
Apply results to decisions and monitor performance.
For ARIMA-family models: check stationarity and differencing, use ACF/PACF to inform model structure, and test whether residuals resemble white noise.
