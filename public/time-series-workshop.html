Cheat-sheet

The model family, in plain business terms

Keep this open during lecture — same framing as today's slides.

AR — Autoregressive
"Brand momentum"

Today's value depends on its own recent past. High sales last week → likely high this week. ACF decays gradually; PACF cuts off sharply.

MA — Moving Average
"Flash promo shock"

A short-term shock fades over a few periods. ACF cuts off sharply after lag q; PACF decays gradually.

ARIMA
"Brand expansion"

Handles a growing/declining trend by differencing (the "I") until the series is stationary, then applies AR + MA.

SARIMA
"Same pattern, every cycle"

Adds a seasonal AR/I/MA block — ARIMA(p,d,q)(P,D,Q)ₛ — to capture patterns that repeat every s periods (12 = monthly).

ARIMAX
"Advertising drives sales"

Adds an exogenous input X (a leading indicator) on top of Y's own history — often with lags, since the effect of X is delayed.
