Section 1 · Why Decision Intelligence with AI?

Why better decisions matter

Module 1 of 2 · 9 micro‑lessons · ~30 minutes

Welcome

Organisations have never had more data, dashboards or AI tools — yet prioritisation, diagnosis, trade-offs and execution remain hard. This module explores that gap, and how AI shifts from an information partner to a thinking partner, while human judgement stays decisive.

Today's Objectives

  • Understand the decision gap — why more information isn't enough
  • See the Data → Analysis → Decision → Outcome pipeline
  • Experience AI as an information partner, using NotebookLM
  • Experience AI as a thinking partner, using ChatGPT or Gemini
  • Recognise which parts of a decision must stay human‑led
"By the end of this module, you'll be ready to explore what makes AI truly decision-ready, and begin the six-template Decision Journey on the BeautyCo case."

Wrap-Up

Key Takeaways & What's Next

Key Takeaways from Why

  • More data, dashboards and AI tools don't guarantee better decisions
  • Decision Intelligence extends the pipeline: Data → Analysis → Decision → Outcome
  • AI plays two roles — information partner and thinking partner
  • Structured context (not just more prompting) improves AI reasoning
  • Human judgement, ethics and accountability remain decisive

Appendix

Key Terms Made Simple

A plain-language glossary for Module 1

Decision Intelligence
Combining human judgement, evidence and AI reasoning through a structured process to make better decisions.
Information Partner
AI's role when it retrieves, summarises or organises what is already known — e.g. NotebookLM.
Thinking Partner
AI's role when it helps frame questions, generate explanations and evaluate trade-offs — e.g. ChatGPT or Gemini.
Grounded
An AI answer based strictly on supplied sources, rather than general internet knowledge.
Context
The situation, evidence and constraints that make an AI prompt decision-aware rather than generic.
Human-in-the-loop
A process where AI assists, but a person retains final judgement and accountability.
