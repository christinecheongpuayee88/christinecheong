Suggested conclusion (keep collapsed until the workshop debrief)

Diagnosis: Traffic generation is not the primary issue — conversion capability is. Three root causes are confirmed with high or medium confidence: Service Capacity Constraints (High), Inventory Availability Issues (High), Loyalty Programme Weakness (Medium).

Recommended option: Integrated Service + Inventory Improvement — fix conversion capability before driving more traffic.

90-day initiatives: Service Capacity (workforce scheduling, queue management, advisor training) · Inventory Availability (inventory forecasting, automated replenishment, stock visibility) · Loyalty Enhancement (personalized promotions, AI-assisted recommendations, tiered rewards).

Targets: Conversion 18%→22% · CSAT 73→80 · Repeat purchase 30%→35% · Stock-out rate 14%→<8%.

Final insight: Traffic was not the problem. Conversion capability was the problem.
