Retail Banking Case — Group Assignment

Paste a link to any Google Slides/Drive deck below — e.g. the sample-answers deck ↗ for testing, or the real Group Collaborative Space deck ↗ once groups have submitted. Leave it blank to use the sample-answers deck by default. Splits the deck by group and scores each group's shared work against the 4-item rubric below. AI suggestions only — review and finalize yourself.

Dimension1 — Developing2 — Competent3 — Strong
Problem Framing States a vague restatement of the case without a clear Problem, Decision, or Gaps, or conflates the finding with the decision Clearly states the Problem, the Decision the analysis should support, and at least one genuine Gap All three are clear and specific enough to rule out a different valid framing — the Decision names a concrete choice, not just "investigate further"
Information Partner Evidence is asserted without supporting numbers, or numbers are copied without synthesis (a data dump, not a summary) Evidence is organised into clear findings with relevant numbers/percentages, staying descriptive — no causes or hypotheses smuggled in Findings are organised and the group explicitly separates what the data shows from what it might mean — evidence stays evidence
Thinking Partner Only one explanation is offered, or the AI-broadened alternative is never engaged with At least two plausible explanations are considered, with a stated reason why one is better supported by the evidence The chosen explanation explicitly survives a real challenge — an alternative or assumption was tested, and the group can say why it was weakened
Decision-Support Partner A recommendation is given with no confidence level, or perspectives are listed without connecting them to it At least one stakeholder perspective stress-tests the finding, and a recommendation with a stated confidence level is given Multiple perspectives are weighed against each other, a genuine blind spot or trade-off is surfaced, and confidence is justified by naming what evidence would change it
